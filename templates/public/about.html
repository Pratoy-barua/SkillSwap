About SkillSwap

Learn skills. Share knowledge. Grow together.

SkillSwap is a community-driven platform that connects curious learners with verified mentors based on practical skills and location. We make real-world learning collaborative, personal, and accessible to everyone.

Empowering 1-on-1 skill exchange in local communities

The Platform

What is SkillSwap?

SkillSwap was built to bridge the gap between people who want to learn practical, real-world skills and experienced individuals ready to share their expertise. Rather than relying solely on pre-recorded videos, SkillSwap focuses on meaningful, human-to-human learning connections tailored to your schedule, location, and goals.

Whether you want to pick up photography, master spoken English, dive into web development, or develop creative design abilities, SkillSwap gives you the tools to find the right mentor, connect safely, and track your progress from start to finish.

On SkillSwap, you can:

  • ✓ Discover Mentors: Explore active skill categories and find mentors ready to share their expertise.
  • ✓ Search by Skill & Location: Filter by subject, city, and neighborhood for online or local learning.
  • ✓ Review Transparent Profiles: Read verified mentor bios, experience, teaching styles, and reviews.
  • ✓ Direct Communication: Stay in touch through integrated, secure private messaging.
  • ✓ Track Learning Progress: Work through structured milestones with collaborative progress updates.
  • ✓ Community Feedback: Leave authentic ratings and reviews after completing learning journeys.
